Rental Agent

Rental Agent is a personal assistant for a small landlord. It collects the monthly utility bills for the owner's rental houses, splits them among the tenants, and prepares the monthly utilities email and payment reminders for the owner to review and approve.

It is a private, local-first tool: it runs only on the owner's own computer, for the owner's own accounts. It is not a public service and there is nothing to sign up for.

Google account access

With the owner's permission, Rental Agent connects to the owner's Gmail to read bill and payment notification emails (read-only) and to send emails the owner has approved (send-only). See the privacy policy for details.
