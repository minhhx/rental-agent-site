Privacy policy

Effective October 14, 2026.

Rental Agent is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's computer. It is used by its owner alone.

What Google data it accesses

  • Gmail, read-only: utility bill emails and payment notification emails (for example from a bank or a payment app), found by searching for those senders.
  • Gmail, send-only: to send emails the owner has reviewed and approved, such as the monthly utilities email and payment reminders.
  • Email address: to know which account is connected.

How it is used and stored

The data is processed and stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private folder. Sign-in tokens are kept in the computer's secure keychain. Nothing is sent to any server operated by Rental Agent, and there are no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models. Rental Agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The owner can delete the stored data at any time by deleting the app's data folder, and can revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
